Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Clip Factory is a personal desktop application. It runs only on its owner's computer and is used by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app uses

  • When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Google gives the app an access token. The app uses it only to upload videos to that channel and to set their titles, descriptions and the channel's appearance.
  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system's secure keychain. They are never sent anywhere except to Google's own servers.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it has no analytics or advertising.

YouTube API Services

Clip Factory uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google account page Third-party apps with account access. The owner can also delete all stored data by removing the app from their computer.
